About ScholarShayata

First of all, thanks to Lord Krishna for giving me this idea and direction.

I am Harsh Indian, an educator at Hartron, and I created ScholarShayata for students who want to learn and improve their skills but cannot afford high-priced courses.

While teaching, I kept seeing the same problem again and again: serious students were ready to work hard, but pricing blocked their learning path. Many learners from financially limited backgrounds had to skip valuable skill courses even when those skills could change their future.

I also observed that many course bundles are already circulating on Telegram and other online platforms at much lower prices, often half of the original amount or around Rs 400 to Rs 500. Students still try to buy from such places, but many cannot find trusted sources and are frequently trapped in fraud or fake delivery.

My main intention is not to build a money-making business. My first priority is to help students access learning at affordable cost.

I understand this area can raise legal and ethical concerns, and I take that seriously. ScholarShayata is being shaped to stay transparent, reduce fraud for students, and operate in a responsible direction over time.

In simple words: if taking from others is wrong, then exploitation and fraud against students is also wrong. This platform exists to protect learners and make skill education more reachable.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: help students build skills through affordable courses when high prices become a barrier.

Our main priority is to help students, not to make money.

Revenue, payouts, and donations are intended to stay transparent so students can trust the platform. ScholarShayata is designed as a non-profit style initiative focused on access and skill growth.
