# ScholarShayata 🎓

> **Affordable learning. Accessible education. Student-first.**

**ScholarShayata** is a student-first learning platform focused on making skill-based education more accessible and affordable. The platform helps learners discover **free courses, low-cost learning resources, study materials, notes, and practical skill-building opportunities** in one place.

Our goal is simple:

> **Education should be free, or at least affordable enough that cost does not stop a student from learning.**

## 🌐 About ScholarShayata

ScholarShayata is designed for students and learners who want to develop practical skills without being blocked by expensive learning platforms.

The platform brings educational resources into a simple and student-friendly interface where users can:

* 📚 Discover affordable learning resources
* 🎓 Explore skill-based courses
* 📝 Find notes and study materials
* 🆓 Discover free educational resources
* 💰 Find low-cost learning opportunities
* 🔎 Search and filter courses by skills and categories
* 📖 View detailed course information
* 💳 Access supported payment options for eligible resources
* 💬 Contact the ScholarShayata team for assistance
* 🎯 Focus on practical learning instead of certificates

ScholarShayata is built around the idea that **learning outcomes and useful skills matter more than expensive certificates.**

## 🌱 Our Approach

ScholarShayata follows a few simple principles:

### Accessibility

Learning should be accessible to students regardless of their financial background.

### Affordability

We aim to make useful learning resources available at reasonable prices wherever possible.

### Transparency

Students should understand what they are receiving before accessing or purchasing a resource.

### Legitimacy

We aim to work with resources that are legitimately available, authorized, openly licensed, or otherwise permitted for distribution.

### Student First

The platform is designed around the actual learning needs of students.

---

## 🔐 Responsible Content Policy

ScholarShayata does **not** intend to promote unauthorized distribution of copyrighted courses, books, notes, software, or other educational content.

Resources listed on the platform should be:

* Created by the platform or its contributors
* Officially provided by the content owner
* Licensed for redistribution
* Openly available with appropriate permissions
* Or otherwise legally accessible to students

If a copyright owner believes that content has been listed incorrectly, they can contact the ScholarShayata team for review and appropriate action.
